# Third-Party Notices

The generated sheets in `assets/followsprites/` are derived from third-party
Pokemon follower/overworld artwork gathered by the earlier Wilds project.
They remain under the rights and licensing terms of their original authors
and are not claimed as original artwork by this rewrite.

The source lineage includes Followers EX / PokePC Followers, ShockSlayer's
Pokemon Crystal Clear artwork, HGSS/PokeMMO-style community sprites, and gap
fillers credited to their respective artists. This package is intended for
free, non-commercial fan use only.

The normalized sheets in `assets/hgsssprites/` are derived from the HGSS /
PokeMMO-style atlas shipped with Wilds of Kanto v2.7.2. They retain the same
community-art ownership and licensing constraints.

The sheets in `assets/swimsprites/` are exact per-species extractions from the
original Wilds of Kanto v2.7.0 water-swimming atlas. They retain the original
follow-sprite/overworld-art ownership and licensing constraints.

Gen1Recomp and Pokemon are owned by their respective projects and rights
holders. No ROM data is included in this mod.

## Untamed Advanced engine and atlas

main.lua, follower.lua, owe.lua, data.lua, atlas.png and palettes.png derive
from the user-supplied Untamed Advanced 1.0.0-beta.5 archive by
[goldenroddeptstore](https://github.com/goldenroddeptstore/Untamed-Advanced).
They retain that project's source and artwork attribution and applicable terms.
The grass base rendering change in main.lua is ported from the user-supplied
Untamed Advanced 1.0.0-beta.6 release by the same author.
Its source lineage includes pret/pokeemerald-expansion's followers and overworld
wild encounter implementation and Emerald Rogue's encounter chaining.
This integration changes actor counts, stationary idle effects, options, collision
and 1025Dex policy; it is not represented as an unchanged upstream build.
The nineteen appended national sheets derive from the existing WildFollowers
HGSS/PokeMMO-style assets described above.

## G9RP and swimming sprites

assets/swim.png packs the original Wilds of Kanto swimming sheets without
changing their frame pixels. It retains the source ownership and credits
listed above. assets/g9rpsprites contains the unchanged original G9RP PNGs
from the user's previous WildFollowers release, with its community artwork
rights and applicable terms retained. None of this third-party artwork is
claimed as original work by this integration. The former standalone G/S/C
and HG/SS packs are no longer included or selectable.
