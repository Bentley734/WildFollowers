# Emerald Expansion artwork

Based off RHH's pokeemerald-expansion 1.17.1 https://github.com/rh-hideout/pokeemerald-expansion/

Credit to RHH (RomHackingHideout), the creators, maintainers and contributing artists. Full contributor attribution: https://github.com/rh-hideout/pokeemerald-expansion/blob/master/CREDITS.md

The user-supplied Emerald Expansion Sprites folder provides the indexed overworld art and JASC palettes. The converter changes storage format and directional sheet layout; it does not redraw the artwork. EE-OVERWORLD-AUDIT.json lists every source PNG, palette and output digest. The existing G9 artwork retains its separate notices in SUPPLIED-ARTWORK-NOTICES.md.
