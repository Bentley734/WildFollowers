# Third-party notices

The follower state machine derives from the user-supplied Untamed Advanced sources by [goldenroddeptstore](https://github.com/goldenroddeptstore/Untamed-Advanced), carried forward through WildFollowers. The source lineage includes pret/pokeemerald-expansion's follower implementation. This add-on changes follower counts, idle effects, settings, collision and rendering integration; it is not an unchanged upstream release.

Untamed Advanced itself, its wild engine and its native atlas/palettes are required at runtime and are not bundled in this package.

`assets/g9rpsprites/` contains the unchanged original G9RP PNGs from the user's earlier WildFollowers release. Their community artwork ownership, credits and applicable terms remain with the original artists; this add-on does not claim that artwork as original.
