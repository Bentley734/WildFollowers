# Alternate-form overworld artwork

EE artwork remains based off RHH's pokeemerald-expansion 1.17.1:
https://github.com/rh-hideout/pokeemerald-expansion/
See EE-ARTWORK-CREDITS.md for its existing attribution and conversion details.

New G9RP form art comes from Caruban's Generation 9 Resource Pack 3.3.8:
https://eeveeexpo.com/resources/1101/
The original pack credits are reproduced unchanged in G9RP-SOURCE-CREDITS.txt.

- New Mega sprites: DarkusShadow, BluebirdDxD.
- Resource preparation and resizing: Willøw.

WildFollowers adapts these fan-made sheets to its four-direction animation
layout, restores clipped transparent canvas edges and records alpha bounds for
the selectable water crop. No battle sprites, scripts or ROM data from the
downloaded resources are included. FORM-ARTWORK-AUDIT.json records each imported
image's source archive/member, source hash, output hash and transformations.
